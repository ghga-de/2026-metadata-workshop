GHGA Experimental Metadata Workshop

Reflecting on the Experimental Metadata Ingress Model — looking ahead to the next phase

8–10 July 2026 Three days · lunch to lunch Berlin · Rahel Hirsch Center

About this meeting

A three-day consortium meeting to reconnect on the GHGA Experimental Metadata Ingress Model (EMIM), share experiences from across the federated Data Hubs, and explore directions for its continued evolution as we approach the next major ETL release.

Goals

Resume the conversation

Restart consortium-wide dialogue on the EMIM and welcome new colleagues into the discussion.

Bridge two perspectives

Build shared understanding between the EMIM development team and the Data Stewards working at the interface with experimentalists in the Core Units.

Sketch the path ahead

Surface candidate directions for the EMIM going forward and explore whether a working group can carry the work through the coming year.

Location

The meeting will take place in central Berlin on the Charité Mitte campus.
